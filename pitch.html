The Competition
競爭對手

Investors have already bet $20.8M on AI coaching. All of it on skis.

這個想法已經被投過 $20.8M，只是投在另一塊板子上。

Slopes ↗

The "Strava of skiing." Started by one developer, still bootstrapped, and on track to pass $5M in revenue in 2025 ($3.6M booked by June) with 4M+ downloads to date. Why does simple tracking make that much money? Because it's a habit: riders check their speed and vertical on every lift, then share the stats card afterward. Slopes proves two things that matter here: riders pay for snow software, and tracking gets the app opened every day. The one thing it can't do: tell you how to ride better.

滑雪界的 Strava。一人起家、至今沒拿創投，2025 年營收上看 $5M（6 月已達 $3.6M），累計下載超過 400 萬。為什麼「純記錄」能賺這麼多？因為它是習慣：雪友每趟纜車都打開看速度和落差，再把成績卡分享給朋友。Slopes 替我們證明了兩件事：雪友願意為滑雪軟體付錢，而「記錄」正是讓人每天打開 App 的鉤子。但它到今天還做不到：告訴你怎麼滑得更好。

Carv ↗

The AI ski coach. Raised $20.8M and proved skiers will pay for AI coaching. The catch: it takes a $249-a-year hardware membership with sensors in your ski boots, and ten years in, it still doesn't work for snowboards.

AI 滑雪教練。募了 $20.8M，證明雪友願意為 AI 教練付費。但它的教練功能綁在硬體會員上（含雪靴感測器，年費 $249），而且做了十年，到今天完全不支援單板。

What A/B testing found
A/B 測試發現

Two creatives, same budget (NT$370 each), same audience, 2 days. B beat A on every metric: 74% more site visits (106 vs 61), a 53% vs 22% three-second hold rate, and roughly half the cost per visit. B's hook now leads the winter creative plan. We do not guess; we test, then scale winners.

兩支素材、相同預算（各 NT$370）、相同受眾、跑 2 天。B 全面勝出：到站多 74%（106 vs 61）、前三秒留存 53% vs 22%、每次到站成本約一半。B 的開場 hook 將主導雪季素材。我們不猜，測完放大贏家。
