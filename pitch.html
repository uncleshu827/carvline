The Competition
競爭對手

Investors have already bet US$20.8M on AI coaching. All of it on skis.

這個想法已經被投過 US$20.8M，只是投在另一塊板子上。

Slopes ↗

The "Strava of skiing." One independent developer, no venture money, and by 2025 roughly US$5M a year in revenue with 4M+ downloads. Why does simple tracking make that much money? Because it's a habit: riders check their speed and vertical on every lift, then share the stats card afterward. Slopes proves two things we build on: riders pay for snow software, and tracking gets the app opened every day. The one thing it can't do: tell you how to ride better.

滑雪界的 Strava。一位獨立開發者、沒拿創投，到 2025 年做到年營收約 US$5M、超過 400 萬下載。為什麼「純記錄」能賺這麼多？因為它是習慣：雪友每趟纜車都打開看速度和落差，再把成績卡分享給朋友。Slopes 證明了我們踩在上面的兩件事：雪友願意為滑雪軟體付錢，而「記錄」正是讓人每天打開 App 的鉤子。但它到今天還做不到：告訴你怎麼滑得更好。

Carv ↗

The AI ski coach. Raised US$20.8M and proved skiers will pay for AI coaching. The catch: you have to buy a US$200+ sensor for your ski boots, and ten years in, it still doesn't support snowboarding at all.

AI 滑雪教練。募了 US$20.8M，證明雪友願意為 AI 教練付費。但你得先買一個 US$200+ 的雪靴感測器，而且做了十年，到今天完全不支援單板。

Business Model
商業模式

Priced like a season pass: US$25 a winter, ~85% gross margin.

像季票一樣收費：一季 US$25，毛利約 85%。

Uploading a clip is free and gets you 3 quick takeaways. The full 8-point analysis and drill plan is what you pay for. Today it's US$1 per unlock as a test price. At winter launch it becomes a season pass:

任何人都能免費上傳影片、拿到 3 個重點回饋。完整的 8 項分析＋訓練計畫才需要付費。現在是 US$1 解鎖的測試價；雪季正式上線後改成季票制：

Why it works: riders already spend hundreds on lift passes and gear every season. US$25 is less than 20 minutes with a human coach. No hardware means ~85% gross margin, and shared highlight reels keep bringing in new riders for free.

為什麼行得通：雪友每季本來就花好幾百美元買雪票和裝備，US$25 還不到真人教練 20 分鐘的價錢。零硬體代表約 85% 毛利，而用戶分享的精華短片會持續免費帶進新雪友。

And the off-season? Snow apps flatline from April to November. Even Slopes does. Our long-term answer is indoor snow: China alone logged 5.6M indoor ski visits in 2024/25 (+15%), has 7 of the world's 10 biggest indoor slopes, and runs year-round. That's the roadmap to a four-season business.

那淡季怎麼辦？雪類 App 每年 4–11 月都是平的，連 Slopes 也一樣。我們的長期解法是室內雪場：光中國 2024/25 就有 560 萬室內滑雪人次（年增 15%），全球前十大室內雪場有 7 座在中國，而且全年營業。這是把生意做成四季的路線圖。
